Biodynamic Breathwork, international, 2025

173 practitioners on a map, and a mobile score that went from 39 to 92.

ProblemA worldwide training body needed to publish and maintain practitioner profiles, and the site was failing PageSpeed on phones.
BuiltA Team Members post type with photo, city and coordinates, a Leaflet map fed by those records, then a caching and image pass.
ResultEditors add a person without touching a layout. PageSpeed moved from 83 and 39 to 98 and 92 at hand over.
